# CitizenSolar Light Paper

**Storage-first infrastructure for the software-defined grid.**

Status: Canon draft. Short investor/customer surface.

## One-Liner

CitizenSolar is a software-led infrastructure company for the storage-first grid.

It turns solar-storage complexity into jurisdiction-fit Energy System Packs: compliant hardware, secure telemetry, deterministic orchestration, supplier-risk screening, finance-fit evidence, installer/EPC handoff, and Community Energy Bank reporting.

## Problem

The grid is becoming distributed, storage-heavy, cyber-physical, finance-constrained, and software-operated.

Buyers do not only need panels or batteries. They need a complete package that answers:

- Which hardware is supportable?
- Which suppliers are acceptable?
- Which grants, loans, leases, rebates, or tax-credit routes might apply?
- Which installation and interconnection path is realistic?
- Which telemetry and cybersecurity posture is acceptable?
- Which operating model is safe, auditable, and governable?
- Which community or public-benefit reports can be produced after deployment?

## Solution

CitizenSolar packages this into Energy System Packs.

Each pack combines:

- screened storage-first hardware;
- SolarINT software layer;
- BYOS adapter posture;
- supplier-risk screen;
- finance-fit record;
- beneficiary compliance screen;
- installer/EPC handoff;
- deterministic orchestration boundary;
- Community Energy Bank readiness.

## What We Sell First

1. Finance-Fit Energy System Review
2. Orchestration Readiness Review
3. Energy System Pack design
4. Supplier/EPC quote routing and project handoff
5. Monitoring/reporting software subscription
6. Community Energy Bank reporting where applicable

## Why Now

Storage, grid congestion, cybersecurity rules, supplier-origin scrutiny, public funding requirements, and distributed-energy adoption are converging into one buyer problem. CitizenSolar turns that problem into a repeatable system.

## Moat

CitizenSolar is not a single battery, inverter, EMS, DERMS, or VPP product.

The moat is the packaging layer:

```text
jurisdiction + finance + supplier risk + orchestration + telemetry + CEB governance
```

## First Wedges

- Bulgaria municipal Community Energy Bank pilots
- EU SME reserve and weak-grid resilience packs
- Netherlands grid-congestion storage packs
- Texas industrial backup packs

## Boundary

CitizenSolar does not provide final legal, tax, accounting, procurement, engineering, interconnection, cybersecurity-certification, funding-approval, savings-guarantee, grid-revenue-guarantee, or live regulated market-dispatch authority.

## Investor Thesis

CitizenSolar starts as a services-plus-software infrastructure packager and can grow toward recurring software, compliance intelligence, supplier-risk intelligence, Community Energy Bank reporting, orchestration readiness tooling, and eventually regional hardware/controller production.
