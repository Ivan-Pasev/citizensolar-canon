# CitizenSolar Canon RC0.1

**Release state:** RC0.1 public doctrine layer.

This file is the top-level canonical doctrine for CitizenSolar. It defines what the project is, what it is not, which claims are allowed, and how downstream website and implementation repositories inherit the public boundary.

## 1. Canonical Identity

**CS-CLAIM-001 — Identity**

CitizenSolar is a storage-first distributed energy infrastructure platform for the software-defined grid.

**CS-CLAIM-002 — Function**

CitizenSolar packages compliant solar-storage systems using trusted hardware, secure telemetry, deterministic orchestration, supplier-risk screening, finance-readiness evidence, and Community Energy Bank reporting.

**CS-CLAIM-003 — Not a reseller**

CitizenSolar is not a generic battery reseller. It is a jurisdiction-fit, finance-aware, sovereign energy-system packager and secured deterministic orchestration layer for storage-first distributed grids.

## 2. Strategic Category

**Public category:**

```text
Secured Deterministic Grid Orchestration
```

**Technical category:**

```text
Causal Agentic Orchestration for Cyber-Physical Energy Infrastructure
```

**Procurement-safe phrase:**

```text
Operator-supervised orchestration agents for storage, telemetry, reserve policy, supplier-risk, and Community Energy Bank operations.
```

## 3. Storage-First Thesis

**CS-CLAIM-004 — Storage-first market thesis**

Storage is not an accessory to solar. Storage is the operating layer of the next distributed grid.

This claim is supported by current storage-growth, grid-congestion, and flexibility evidence, but it does not imply guaranteed CitizenSolar revenue, savings, market access, permit approval, interconnection approval, or public funding.


## 5. Mandatory Pack Scores

**CS-CLAIM-006 — Five-score gate**

Every serious Energy System Pack carries:

- `CS-PROVIDER-FIT`
- `CS-SOV-STACK`
- `CS-FINANCE-FIT`
- `CS-ORCHESTRATION-FIT`
- `CS-CEB-FIT`
- beneficiary compliance screen
- bounded claim review

Scores are readiness classifications for review, planning, and partner handoff. They are not approvals, certifications, funding guarantees, tax-credit qualifications, engineering approvals, procurement approvals, savings guarantees, or grid-revenue guarantees.

## 6. Current Action Boundary

**CS-CLAIM-007 — Orchestration action boundary**

The public orchestration boundary is:

```text
observe → explain → recommend → simulate → operator review → audit
```

Supervised execution is staged and bounded. Autonomous market action is future-only and disabled unless separately permitted, implemented, reviewed, governed, and certified where required.

## 7. Community Energy Bank Boundary

**CS-CLAIM-008 — Community Energy Bank**

The Community Energy Bank is a governed shared-storage operating model with seven ledgers:

1. Asset Ledger
2. Capacity Ledger
3. Reserve-Policy Ledger
4. Dispatch/Event Ledger
5. Allocation-Report Ledger
6. Assurance Ledger
7. Governance Ledger

It is not a deposit-taking bank, investment product, speculative token system, guaranteed-return product, or regulated settlement service.

## 8. Finance-Fit Boundary

**CS-CLAIM-009 — Finance-Fit**

Finance-Fit Energy System Review maps grants, loans, leases, tax-credit evidence, supplier-risk screening, beneficiary compliance, ownership options, and readiness evidence for storage-first deployment.

It is a bounded readiness and packaging service. It does not provide tax advice, legal advice, accounting advice, procurement advice, final engineering advice, funding approval, grant awards, rebate confirmation, tax-credit qualification, loan approval, permit approval, interconnection approval, savings guarantee, or revenue guarantee.

## 9. Supplier Boundary

**CS-CLAIM-010 — Supplier candidates**

Supplier inclusion means candidate or screened status only unless a documented partnership exists. It does not mean approval, certification, procurement approval, funding eligibility, endorsement, or final compatibility.

## 10. Repo Boundary Rule

**CS-CLAIM-011 — Canon upstream authority**

The public canon may define doctrine and architecture. It must not contain private firmware, production control logic, deployment secrets, private supplier diligence, customer data, live trading/settlement systems, or security-sensitive implementation internals.

**CS-CLAIM-012 — Downstream implementation hold**

Implementation repositories inherit this canon and remain downstream until RC0.1 review is accepted.

## 11. Canon-First Gate

Before implementation repos move forward, the canon must define:

- public White Paper;
- technical Blue Paper;
- investor/customer Light Paper;
- claim boundaries;
- stable claim IDs;
- source register;
- maturity ladder;
- repository ecosystem map;
- release gates;
- diagram backlog.

## 12. RC0.1 Closure

RC0.1 closes the public doctrine skeleton. It does not close final website synchronization, certification, technical implementation, production hardware, regulated market integration, token infrastructure, or live P2P settlement.
