## Source Classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SOURCE-LIVE` | Derived from citizen.solar public pages |
| `SOURCE-OFFICIAL` | Government, regulator, standards body, IRS/DOE/EU/IEA etc. |
| `SOURCE-MARKET` | Market research, analyst, industry source |
| `SOURCE-INFERENCE` | Strategic synthesis from multiple sources |
| `SOURCE-INTERNAL` | Internal working doctrine, not external evidence |

## Competitive Evidence Classes

Track but do not overclaim against:

- DERMS vendors;
- VPP/flexibility platforms;
- BESS integrators;
- EMS/energy management systems;
- solar marketplaces;
- solar design/quoting software;
- public-sector resilience platforms;
- supplier-risk/compliance tools.

## Refresh Rule

Refresh before:

- investor release;
- funding submission;
- procurement discussion;
- public route update;
- supplier candidate publication;
- finance-fit customer delivery;
- website metadata change;
- any claim about current regulation, incentives, market size, suppliers, or competitor status.

Do not treat old market/regulatory claims as permanent. Mark old values as dated and replace with current evidence when new data exists.
