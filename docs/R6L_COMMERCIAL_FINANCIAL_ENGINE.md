## Purpose

R6L converts Canon RC0.1 from investor-readiness into a first sellable commercial mechanism without promoting assumptions into validated economics.

## First paid offer

**CitizenSolar Finance-Fit + Orchestration Readiness Review**

Planning bands:
- Starter: €2,500
- Standard: €7,500
- Complex: €15,000

The review can include finance-fit mapping, supplier-risk screening, five-score readiness, orchestration-readiness assessment, Energy System Pack outline, and monitored-pilot conversion path.

## Bottom-up model

Three cases are explicitly separated:

1. **Case A — Services Only**
2. **Case B — Services + Partner Margin**
3. **Case C — Pilot + Recurring Software**

Current editable RC0.1 assumptions include qualified-lead volume, review conversion, delivery labour, deployment attach, routed-system value, partner margin, commissions, software attach, setup fees, subscriptions, churn and fixed cost.

### Current result

Under the current assumptions, **none of the three cases reaches EBITDA break-even within the first three years**.

This finding must not be cosmetically normalized away.

Indicative annual result from the current model:

| Case | Y1 Revenue | Y1 EBITDA | Y3 Revenue | Y3 EBITDA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A — Services Only | €180k | -€138k | €180k | -€138k |
| B — Services + Partner Margin | €315k | -€128.25k | €315k | -€128.25k |
| C — Pilot + Recurring Software | ~€431.7k | ~-€139.8k | ~€518.3k | ~-€53.2k |

The model therefore acts as an assumption-testing instrument, not a fundraising profitability claim.

## Evidence state

Current external evidence supports market timing and regulatory/finance screening, not CitizenSolar revenue forecasts. Current anchors include IEA 2026 battery-storage deployment data, the EU Modernisation Fund, and the Cyber Resilience Act timeline.

## RC0.2 promotion gate

Do **not** promote current pricing, attach rates, partner margin, recurring software revenue, or CEB reporting revenue from assumption to validated economics until there is evidence from:

1. at least one paid review or signed pilot / LOI;
2. partner commercial terms where partner margin is modeled;
3. monitored real-device telemetry integration;
4. delivery-time and labour evidence;
5. actual customer acquisition / conversion data.

Implementation repositories remain downstream and on HOLD until the commercial and site-sync gates are satisfied.
