# CitizenSolar R6M — First-Customer Acquisition Engine

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: CLOSED at RC0.1 / RC0.2 commercial-validation gate remains HOLD.

## Purpose
R6M converts the R6L paid offer into an operational acquisition system without changing CitizenSolar's bounded public claims.

## Operating rule
A named organization in the CRM is a **research target**, not a qualified lead, customer, partner or endorsement. Qualification requires an active problem, a data path, a named decision/budget owner, timing evidence and a CS-LEAD-SCORE.

## CS-LEAD-SCORE / 100
- Problem intensity: 0–20
- Data readiness: 0–15
- Decision access: 0–15
- Finance relevance: 0–15
- Storage fit: 0–15
- Orchestration fit: 0–10
- Timing: 0–10

Bands: 80–100 Tier A; 60–79 Tier B; 40–59 Tier C; below 40 hold/research.

## Commercial conversion path
Research target → qualification → complete intake → scoped SOW → paid Finance-Fit + Orchestration Readiness Review → evidence-gap closure → partner RFQ/EPC pathway → monitored pilot → recurring software only after pilot evidence.

## RC0.2 gate
Promotion requires at least one paid readiness review or a signed pilot/LOI with named budget owner, site/data access and decision window. Actual sales-cycle duration, delivery effort, realized fee, objection log and conversion evidence must be captured. No recurring revenue or partner margin is treated as validated before contracts/pilot evidence.

## Boundary
No guaranteed funding, savings, tax treatment, permits, procurement results, autonomous dispatch, regulated settlement or supplier approval may be asserted through the acquisition workflow.
